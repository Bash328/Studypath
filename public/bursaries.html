Money for studying that you don't pay back

Bursaries, sorted by what closes first

The single biggest reason students miss out on funding isn't their marks — it's a deadline that went past while they were busy with school. So we sort by the closing date, and we'll WhatsApp you before yours arrives.

New to this? Here's the short version

A bursary is money for your studies that you usually don't pay back — unlike a student loan. Some come with a condition, like working for the company for a couple of years after you graduate.

Most bursaries close mid-year, before you even write matric, and they want your Grade 11 results. If you wait until December, you've missed almost all of them.

How we pick what goes on this page

The same rule as the rest of Studypath: we only list a bursary once we have its closing date and its conditions from the provider's own page, and we link to that page so you can check us. Bursary scams are real, and a list of unverified "opportunities" would do more harm than an empty page.
