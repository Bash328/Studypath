Where this data comes from

One rule: if a requirement isn't on an official university page or PDF, it doesn't go on Studypath. Not "probably", not "usually", not "according to another site". This page shows you what we have, how each university counts, and — just as importantly — what we still don't know.

The rules we hold ourselves to

Every number links to its source

Not in a tooltip. Not behind a "learn more". A visible link, next to the number, on every single programme. If you ever find a figure here without one, that's a bug — tell us.

We never put universities on one scale

Their scoring systems measure different things. Combining or comparing them would produce a number that is confidently wrong, which is the worst kind. So the calculator gives you a separate result per university, every time.

When sources disagree, we show both

Wits' Bachelor of Architectural Studies is the clearest example: the schools-liaison guide says APS 34, the programme's own FAQ says 29. We show a "Sources disagree" badge and both figures rather than quietly picking one.

When we can't compute something, we say so

We don't calculate a UKZN, UWC, NWU or UFS score, because we haven't verified those universities' formulas from their own pages. Guessing would give you a number to plan your life around that we made up.

The dataset grows, it doesn't pretend to be finished

South Africa has 26 public universities. We publish one only once it's verified, and we add more twice a week. You'll see gaps here because there are gaps.

What we could not verify

Most sites hide this. We think a student deciding their future deserves to know exactly where the information runs out. Every entry below is something we tried to confirm and couldn't, with the reason.

Found something wrong?

Universities change their requirements, and documents get replaced. If a figure here doesn't match what the university's own page says today, the university is right and we are wrong — please tell us so we can fix it.
