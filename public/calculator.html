What do your marks actually get you?

Put in your subjects and your latest percentages. We'll work out your score at each university the way that university does it, and show you which degrees you qualify for.

Before you start — two things worth knowing

Use your most recent real marks. Your Grade 11 final or latest Grade 12 report is fine. Most universities let you apply on Grade 11 results.

There is no single "APS". Each university counts different subjects in a different way, so you'll get a separate score for each one. That's not a bug — putting them on one scale would give you the wrong answer.

Your subjects and marks

Add every subject you take, including Life Orientation — some universities count it and some don't, and we handle that for you. Percentages, not levels.

How to read your results

✓ You meet the published requirements

Your score and every subject requirement we can check are satisfied. If the programme is also marked Selection programme, that still doesn't guarantee a place — the university chooses from everyone who qualified.

You are close

You're within a few points, or a few percent in one subject. These are the ones worth pushing for this year — we tell you exactly what's missing, like "2% to go in Maths".

We cannot tell you yet

These need something we can't work out from marks alone — an NBT result, a portfolio, an audition, or a cut-off the university simply doesn't publish. We'd rather say so than guess.
