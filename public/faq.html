Marks & APS

What is an APS, and is it the same at every university?

APS stands for Admission Point Score: a number a university works out from your marks to decide whether you meet its entry requirements. It is not the same at every university - each one counts different subjects in a different way, so a score from one university means nothing at another.

✓ Checked Source: How each university scores you (Studypath)

A few examples. UCT adds your six best subject percentages (English is always counted; anything under 40% scores nothing). Wits uses a points scale where English and Maths get a bonus and Life Orientation counts a little. UP adds the levels (1 to 7) of your six best subjects and leaves out Life Orientation. Stellenbosch uses your average percentage and no APS at all. Rhodes adds your six subjects’ percentages and divides by 10.

✓ Checked Sources: UCT Guidelines for Admission for NSC holders (2025) (opens the official page) · Wits undergraduate entry requirements (opens the official page) · UP Faculty of Humanities admission document (opens the official page) · Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page) · Rhodes Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

That is why Studypath works out a separate score for each university from the same marks, and never shows you one "APS".

✓ Checked Source: How each university scores you (Studypath)

Where to check this yourself
  • The "how to calculate your APS" page on the university’s own admissions site
Is UCT’s score out of 600, 800 or 900?

It depends on the faculty. For Commerce, Engineering & the Built Environment, Humanities and Law your Faculty Points Score (FPS) is out of 600 and equals your APS. For Science it is out of 800 (your Maths and Physical Sciences are counted twice). For Health Sciences it is out of 900 (your APS plus your NBT results).

✓ Checked Source: UCT Guidelines for Admission for NSC holders (2025) (opens the official page)

A worked example from UCT’s own guidelines: English 75, isiXhosa 70, Maths 84, Physical Sciences 86, CAT 79 and Sports & Exercise Science 69 give a Commerce/Engineering/Humanities/Law FPS of 463/600, and for Science the same marks give 633/800. Studypath reproduces both numbers.

✓ Checked Source: UCT Guidelines for Admission for NSC holders (2025) (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • UCT’s "Guidelines for admission for NSC holders" (opens the official page)
Does Life Orientation count towards my APS?! Not from an official source

It depends on the university. UCT, UP, UJ, UKZN, NWU and Stellenbosch leave it out of the score. Wits counts it, but only for a little: 4, 3, 2 or 1 point for 90, 80, 70 or 60% and up. UFS gives 1 point for 60% or more. UWC gives 0 to 3 points. Rhodes does not score it but you must get at least 50% for acceptance.

✓ Checked Sources: UCT Guidelines for Admission for NSC holders (2025) (opens the official page) · Wits undergraduate entry requirements (opens the official page) · UP Faculty of Humanities admission document (opens the official page) · UKZN College of Law & Management Studies Handbook 2026 (opens the official page) · NWU APS calculator (opens the official page) · Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page) · UFS Undergraduate Programmes 2024 (AP score) (opens the official page) · UWC official APS calculator (opens the official page) · Rhodes Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

Even where it does not count, most universities still expect you to pass it, so do not ignore it.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • The "admission requirements" page of the university you are applying to
What are achievement levels (1 to 7)?

Your percentage in each subject is turned into an NSC achievement level from 1 to 7: 7 = 80-100%, 6 = 70-79%, 5 = 60-69%, 4 = 50-59%, 3 = 40-49%, 2 = 30-39%, 1 = 0-29%. When a university asks for "Maths level 6" it means 70% or more.

✓ Checked Source: UP Faculty of Humanities admission document (opens the official page)

A few universities (Wits, UKZN, NWU, UWC and UFS) split the top band so that 90-100% is level 8 and 80-89% is level 7.

✓ Checked Sources: Wits undergraduate entry requirements (opens the official page) · UKZN College of Law & Management Studies Handbook 2026 (opens the official page) · NWU APS calculator (opens the official page) · UWC official APS calculator (opens the official page) · UFS Undergraduate Programmes 2024 (AP score) (opens the official page)

What is the difference between Home Language and First Additional Language?! Not from an official source

Your Home Language is the language subject you study at the higher level; your First Additional Language is the second language subject, studied at a lower level. Every NSC learner takes both.

! Not from an official source

Because the First Additional Language is the easier subject, universities often ask for a higher mark in it. For example, UCT Commerce asks for English Home Language 50% or English First Additional Language 60%.

◔ From our research Source: UCT 2027 Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • Your school’s subject-choice or guidance teacher
  • Department of Basic Education (education.gov.za) (opens the official page)
If I meet the minimum requirements, will I get a place?

No. Rhodes says plainly: "obtaining the minimum requirements does not guarantee acceptance." UWC’s own calculator adds that faculties "may select at higher point scores and subject levels than the published minimums."

✓ Checked Sources: Rhodes Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page) · UWC official APS calculator (opens the official page)

Many degrees are selection programmes: the university has more qualified applicants than places, so it picks the strongest. Stellenbosch, for example, says its real selection threshold is higher than the published minimum. Studypath marks these with a "Selection programme" badge.

✓ Checked Source: Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page)

Which marks do I use - Grade 11 or Grade 12?◔ From our research

If you are in Grade 12, universities use your final Grade 11 results to decide on your application, and your final NSC results decide the final offer. UP says so directly, and UCT counts Grade 11 final, Grade 12 preliminary and Grade 12 final marks.

✓ Checked Sources: UP Faculty of Humanities admission document (opens the official page) · UCT Guidelines for Admission for NSC holders (2025) (opens the official page)

So apply in Grade 12 using your Grade 11 finals, and check each programme’s own closing date.

◔ From our research Source: Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page)

The NBT

What is the NBT?

The National Benchmark Tests "measure your academic readiness for University." There are two tests. The AQL (Academic and Quantitative Literacy) is written by applicants to all programmes that require the NBT. The MAT (Mathematics) is for programmes where Mathematics is a requirement. Each is multiple-choice and takes three hours.

✓ Checked Source: NBT Project FAQ (opens the official page)

They are written on the same day - the NBT Project does not let you write the AQL one day and the MAT another - and the MAT cannot be written on its own.

✓ Checked Source: NBT Project FAQ (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • nbt.ac.za (opens the official page)
Which universities and faculties require the NBT?! Not from an official source

It is set by each university and each faculty, not nationally. Stellenbosch does not require it for 2027 except for Law programmes, the School for Tomorrow, AHSD and online-school applicants: LLB needs the AQL, and BCom (Law) and BAccLLB need the AQL and MAT, all written before 31 July.

✓ Checked Source: Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page)

UCT requires it for South African resident applicants and for all Health Sciences and Commerce applicants. UCT Engineering & the Built Environment makes you write all three parts but says the results "are not taken into account for admission".

◔ From our research Source: UCT 2027 Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

Wits requires it for all Health Sciences and Science applicants, and Health Sciences applicants must write in person.

◔ From our research Sources: Wits course finder: Medicine and Surgery (opens the official page) · Wits course finder: BSc (opens the official page)

Rhodes "recommended[s] that all first-time university-entering South African applicants write the NBT". If you do not meet the automatic entry requirements, your results feed into the Dean’s decision. Everyone should write the AQL; the MAT is for Commerce, Pharmacy and Science applicants.

✓ Checked Source: Rhodes Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

At UP’s Faculty of Humanities, a Grade 11 APS of 26-29 means you must write the NBT, and your result can place you in the BA Extended programme; the NBT is not required for all selection degrees.

✓ Checked Source: UP Faculty of Humanities admission document (opens the official page)

Websites that say "most universities require the NBT" are too vague to rely on: always check the faculty page of the degree you want.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • The admission-requirements page for your degree at the university
  • The NBT Project (opens the official page)
When can I write the NBT, and can I write it again?◔ From our research

"The NBTs are available from May until the first Saturday in January." You book a date and venue on nbt.ac.za and can change the booking until that sitting’s closing date.

✓ Checked Source: NBT Project FAQ (opens the official page)

You may write the NBT twice within a cycle, but you pay the full fee both times. Not every university will accept a second score, so ask before you rewrite.

✓ Checked Source: NBT Project FAQ (opens the official page)

Universities set their own deadline for receiving your results, and these can be as early as June or July. UCT says you do not need to rewrite if your NBTs were written in the last three years.

◔ From our research Source: UCT applications & admissions FAQ (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • nbt.ac.za - the test schedule (opens the official page)
How much does the NBT cost?! Not from an official source

We have not confirmed the current fee. The NBT FAQ we read does not state an amount, so we are not going to guess one. The fee is shown when you book.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • Book a test at nbt.ac.za - the fee is shown before you pay (opens the official page)

Applying

When do applications open and close?! Not from an official source

It varies a lot. For the 2027 intake, for example, UFS closes its health programmes on 31 May, Nursing, Social Work and Architecture on 31 July, and everything else on 30 September. UKZN closes Medicine on 30 June and most other colleges on 30 September. Rhodes ran applications from 1 April to 30 September.

✓ Checked Sources: UFS applications page (opens the official page) · UKZN how to apply (opens the official page) · Rhodes Undergraduate Prospectus (opens the official page)

Others closing later include WSU, UFH and SPU (31 October) and UMP (30 November).

◔ From our research Sources: WSU how to apply (opens the official page) · UFH apply (undergraduate) (opens the official page) · SPU how to apply (opens the official page) · UMP online applications (opens the official page)

Selection programmes such as Medicine and Dentistry usually close earlier than the rest - UFS, UKZN and Wits above all close their health programmes in May or June.

! Not from an official source

Most of these dates have now passed for the 2027 intake. The 2028 dates have not been published, so do not assume they will match - check each university from early next year.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • Our deadlines page, then the university’s own "how to apply" page
What if I do not meet the requirements?◔ From our research

There are options. Some universities have extended or foundation programmes (UCT Science uses NBT results to place students in its extended degree). Diplomas and higher certificates need less than a bachelor’s degree. You can also ask some universities to defer your application to next year (UCT will reconsider you, though deferral "does not mean automatic acceptance"), or rewrite subjects through the Department of Basic Education’s Second Chance Matric Programme.

◔ From our research Source: UCT applications & admissions FAQ (opens the official page)

The national minimum for a bachelor’s degree is 50% in four 20-credit subjects and 30% in the language of teaching; for a diploma it is 40% in four subjects. Universities then add their own subject requirements on top.

◔ From our research Source: Government Gazette 42100 (2018) - the research pack saw it only in secondary copies, not on dhet.gov.za

Where to check this yourself
  • Department of Higher Education and Training (dhet.gov.za) (opens the official page)
  • The university’s "alternative routes" or "extended programme" page
Do I have to send my matric results to the university?! Not from an official source

If you wrote the NSC in South Africa, UCT "will obtain your results directly from your examination authority." Applicants with international qualifications need to send certified results.

◔ From our research Source: UCT applications & admissions FAQ (opens the official page)

Other universities may work differently - check the "documents" section of the application form.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • The "how to apply" page of the university
What is the CAO?◔ From our research

The Central Applications Office (CAO) handles first-time applications to UKZN: the CAO application fee is R250 (non-refundable) and its phone number is +27 (0)31 268 4444.

✓ Checked Source: UKZN how to apply (opens the official page)

Our research also found that first-time applicants to DUT, MUT and UniZulu apply through the CAO.

◔ From our research Sources: DUT how to apply 2027 (opens the official page) · MUT apply (opens the official page) · UniZulu admissions (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • cao.ac.za (opens the official page)
Does it cost money to apply?◔ From our research

It depends on the university. UFS is free. The CAO charges R250 for UKZN applications.

✓ Checked Sources: UFS applications page (opens the official page) · UKZN how to apply (opens the official page)

Reported fees elsewhere: TUT R240, SMU R300, UL R200, UMP R200, UFH R120, WSU R100; CUT is free.

◔ From our research Sources: TUT general information for first-year enrolment (opens the official page) · SMU online application (opens the official page) · UL undergraduate admissions (opens the official page) · UMP online applications (opens the official page) · UFH apply (undergraduate) (opens the official page) · WSU how to apply (opens the official page) · CUT application process (opens the official page)

TUT warns that it "will never charge any prospective student money for making enquiries" and asks you to report scams to its ethics hotline, 0800 006 924.

◔ From our research Source: TUT general information for first-year enrolment (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • Each university’s "how to apply" page

Money

When do I apply for NSFAS?! Not from an official source

⚡ Sources disagree Sources disagree on the NSFAS closing date (18 November vs 31 October). We follow the two government sources.

The NSFAS 2027 application cycle opened on 18 September 2026 and closes on 18 November 2026, with funding outcomes communicated in December. NSFAS is a separate application from applying to a university.

✓ Checked Sources: Minister Buti Manamela: launch of the NSFAS 2027 application cycle (gov.za) (opens the official page) · SAnews: NSFAS 2027 application cycle opens (opens the official page)

We saw a different closing date (31 October) on at least one other website. The Minister’s own launch speech and the SAnews report both say 18 November, so we use that - but always confirm on nsfas.org.za. We have not verified the household-income thresholds, so we do not state one.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • nsfas.org.za - the application page (opens the official page)
Who do I ask about university financial aid?

Each university has its own financial aid office, separate from NSFAS. For example, UCT’s undergraduate financial aid is financialaid@uct.ac.za or +27 (0)21 650 3545.

✓ Checked Source: UCT general contacts (opens the official page)

Where to check this yourself
  • Our "Ask a university" page lists the contact we have for each one

Words explained

What is a "selection programme"?! Not from an official source

A degree where the university has more qualified applicants than places, so meeting the minimum is not enough: it picks the strongest. Stellenbosch puts it this way: it "receives more applications than we have places available. Therefore, meeting the minimum admission requirements does not guarantee admission to your programme of choice. All programmes are selection programmes."

✓ Checked Source: Stellenbosch Admissions Booklet 2027 (opens the official page)

Medicine, dentistry, engineering and law are the usual examples, but every university decides which of its degrees are selection programmes.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • The degree’s page on the university site - look for "selection"
What is the difference between a bursary and a loan?! Not from an official source

A bursary is money for your studies that you normally do not pay back (some come with a condition, such as working for the provider for a set time). A loan must be repaid. These are general definitions - always read the terms of the specific offer.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • The provider’s own terms - and ask the university financial aid office
What is a university of technology?! Not from an official source

South Africa has 26 public universities, and six of them are universities of technology: CPUT, CUT, DUT, MUT, TUT and VUT.

◔ From our research Sources: Universities South Africa (USAf), as quoted in the research pack (opens the official page) · DHET "Staffing South Africa’s Universities Framework" (named in the research pack; page not re-read by us)

Universities of technology (the former technikons) mainly offer career-focused diplomas as well as some degrees; traditional universities mainly offer degrees; comprehensive universities offer both. This description is general knowledge, not quoted from an official page, so we have not labelled any university "traditional" or "comprehensive" as fact.

! Not from an official source

Where to check this yourself
  • Universities South Africa (usaf.ac.za) (opens the official page)
  • Department of Higher Education and Training (dhet.gov.za) (opens the official page)
